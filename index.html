Nhan Duy Tran

Research Collaborator @ URA Research Group, HCMUT

I am a second-year Information Technology student at the Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology (PTIT).

My research focuses on neural machine translation for morphologically rich languages, low-resource languages, and ethnic minority languages.

Google Scholar GitHub LinkedIn ORCID
Research Interests
  • Neural Machine Translation: Building and evaluating translation systems for languages with limited supervision.
  • Morphology-Aware NLP: Understanding how morphological structure can improve tokenization and model adaptation.
  • Low-Resource and Ethnic Minority Languages: Developing inclusive language technologies for Vietnamese ethnic minority communities.
